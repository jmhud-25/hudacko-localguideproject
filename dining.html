Poole's Diner

Located in downtown Raleigh, Poole's Diner is known for its modern take on American comfort food. It is a popular destination for visitors looking for a special dining experience.

  • Explore a creative American menu.

State Farmers Market Restaurant

The State Farmers Market Restaurant is a Raleigh favorite for traditional Southern breakfast and lunch. It is a great place to enjoy a hearty meal before exploring the nearby farmers market.

  • Enjoy classic Southern breakfast dishes.
  • Try a traditional country-style meal.
  • Explore the nearby farmers market.
