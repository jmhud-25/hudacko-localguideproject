Things to Do in Raleigh

Raleigh is full of opportunities to explore, learn, and enjoy the outdoors. Whether you enjoy nature, history, or science, there is something waiting for you.

North Carolina State Capitol

The North Carolina State Capitol is a historic building that houses the state government. Visitors can explore the interior and learn about the history of the state.

  • Take a guided tour of the building.
  • Visit the state legislature in session.
  • Learn about the architecture and design of the Capitol.
Learn more about the State Capitol

Ann and Jim Goodnight Museum Park

Located in the North Carolina Museum of Art, this park provides an opportunity to explore art and nature. Visitors can enjoy outdoor sculptures, walking trails, and beautiful landscapes while learning about the intersection of art and nature.

  • Walk through the park's open spaces and trails.
  • Learn about art and its history.
  • Enjoy a picnic with family and friends.
Visit the museum website

Raleigh Road Outdoor Theater

Raleigh Road Outdoor Theater offers a unique opportunity to enjoy movies in a scenic outdoor setting.

  • Enjoy a movie under the stars.
  • Look into upcoming events the theater is hosting.
  • Take a look at the concession stands.
Visit the theater website
